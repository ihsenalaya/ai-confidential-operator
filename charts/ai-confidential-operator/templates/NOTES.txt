AI Confidential Operator installed as release "{{ .Release.Name }}" in namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}".

This operator owns the confidential-computing CRDs (attestation evidence,
placement decisions, key release, revocation, evidence audit) and hosts the
confidential pod injection/validation webhooks. On kind it bootstraps the
simulated runtime classes automatically.

1. Check the controller is running:
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get deploy {{ include "operator.fullname" . }}

2. Apply a policy and simulated evidence (see the test-apps samples in
   operators/ai-confidential-operator/automatisation/test-apps):
   kubectl apply -f test-apps/

3. Inspect the chain:
   kubectl get cip,rar,aevid,apd,akrp,airvp,aier -A
   kubectl get attestationevidence -o wide

4. Production notes:
   - The dedicated central-verifier binary is the single writer of
     AttestationEvidence; the embedded fallbacks stay disabled by default.
   - Real SEV-SNP/TDX appraisal requires the node-attestation-agent DaemonSet
     and central-verifier deployment (see the confidential platform chart).

the attestation & placement dashboard.
